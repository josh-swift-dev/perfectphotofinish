How to Use

Beautiful photo edits in just a few taps.

Quick Start

  1. Import a photo.
    Open the App and tap Import a Photo. Choose an image from your photo library and allow access if iOS asks.
  2. Try Smart Enhance.
    Open Adjust and tap Smart Enhance. The App analyzes the photo on-device and automatically balances light, color, shadows, highlights, faces, noise, and detail.
  3. Fine-tune if desired.
    Select an adjustment such as Exposure, Contrast, Brightness, Saturation, Vibrance, Highlights, Shadows, Warmth, Sharpness, Clarity, Vignette, Fade, or Grain. Move the slider until the photo looks right.
  4. Crop or transform.
    Choose Crop to select a ratio. You can also rotate the image or flip it horizontally or vertically.
  5. Add a filter.
    Choose Filters and tap a look such as Vivid, Dramatic, Mono, Noir, Warm, Cool, Spotlight, Mosaic, Puzzle, Comic, or Bloom.
  6. Save your photo.
    Tap Save in the upper-right. Choose Save as New Photo to preserve the original, or Overwrite Original to save the edit back to the selected library asset when available.

Zoom and Move

Pinch the photo to zoom. When zoomed in, drag to inspect another area. Double-tap the photo to return to the fitted view. Zooming affects only your view of the canvas, not the saved crop.

Undo, Redo, and Restore

  • Tap the curved left arrow to undo the latest edit.
  • Tap the curved right arrow to redo an edit.
  • Tap Restore, then Yes, to discard all edits and return to the imported photo.

Share Without Saving

Tap the Share icon near the upper-right to open Apple’s share sheet. You can send or export the currently edited image using compatible apps installed on your device.

Best Results

Recommended workflow: Start with Smart Enhance, make small manual changes, choose a filter only if desired, and use Save as New Photo until you are certain you want to update the original.

  • Very large images may take a moment to process.
  • Smart Enhance is intentionally gentler on portraits and warm scenes.
  • If a filter looks too strong, choose Original and continue with manual adjustments.
  • Keep enough free device storage for edited copies.

Privacy

The App does not require an account. Editing and Smart Enhance are designed to occur locally on your device, and your photos are not uploaded to the developer.
