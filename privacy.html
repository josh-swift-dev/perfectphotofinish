Privacy Policy

Studio Photo Editor · Effective September 15, 2026

Privacy summary: No account, no advertising, no subscriptions, and no photo uploads. Photo editing and Smart Enhance processing are designed to occur on your device.

Information We Collect

The App does not require registration and does not ask you to provide a name, mailing address, phone number, location, contacts, or payment details. The developer does not operate an account system or advertising network through the App.

Your Photos

The App accesses only the photos you choose through Apple’s photo picker or the access you authorize in iOS. Selected images are used to provide editing, cropping, filtering, Smart Enhance, sharing, and saving features. The App does not transmit your photos to the developer or to a remote image-processing service.

On-Device Analysis

Smart Enhance may analyze characteristics such as brightness, color, highlights, shadows, noise, scene type, and the presence or location of faces. This analysis is performed using Apple frameworks on your device for the purpose of editing the selected photo. The analysis is not used to identify a person and is not sent to the developer.

Photo Library Permissions

iOS may ask for permission to read selected photos, add edited photos, or update an original photo. You can change Photo access at any time in the Settings app under Privacy & Security or the App’s settings. Restricting access may prevent importing or saving.

Purchases

The one-time App purchase is processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your full payment-card information. Apple’s handling of purchase information is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy.

Analytics, Tracking, and Advertising

The current version of the App does not include third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, or developer-operated analytics. It does not sell or rent personal information.

Data Storage and Retention

Photos remain in locations you control, such as the Photos app or destinations you choose when sharing. Temporary in-memory image data may be used while the App is open. The App does not maintain a developer-accessible cloud library of your photos.

Sharing

When you choose Share, Apple’s share sheet presents destinations available on your device. Any information sent to another app or service is governed by that destination’s privacy practices.

Children’s Privacy

The App is a general-purpose photo utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Keeping processing on-device limits external exposure. No method of device storage or software operation is guaranteed to be completely secure, so users should maintain device security and appropriate backups of important photos.

Policy Changes

This policy may be updated if features or privacy practices change. The effective date will be updated when a revised policy is published.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to josh.swift.dev@gmail.com.
